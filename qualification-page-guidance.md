# Qualification page guidance

## Key purpose of this page

This page is designed to help prospective students quickly understand what the qualification is, what studying it is like, and where it could lead.

Content should focus on the **overall qualification experience** rather than broad subject descriptions or detailed paper-by-paper information.

Focus on:
- What students will study
- Who the qualification is suited to
- What the learning experience is like
- The kinds of careers, further study, or opportunities it may lead to

---

## Tone and style guidance

Content should feel **motivating, practical, and student-focused**. Write in plain English using a clear, welcoming, and confident tone. Keep content specific, relevant, and easy to understand. Use active voice where appropriate and help readers quickly understand the value and experience of the qualification.

**Avoid:**
- Corporate or institutional jargon
- Overly academic or technical language
- Generic marketing claims
- Repeating detailed qualification or paper information already covered elsewhere

---

## Audience guidance

Keep the likely audience in mind:

**Undergraduate students** — approachable, encouraging, and focused on exploration, confidence, belonging, and next steps.

**Postgraduate students** — informed, purposeful, and intellectually grounded, with focus on specialisation, flexibility, research, professional development, and future pathways.

**Parents and supporters** — clear, reassuring, and trustworthy, without making the page feel written primarily for them.

Where audiences overlap, keep the **prospective student as the primary audience** while ensuring content remains clear and credible to others.
